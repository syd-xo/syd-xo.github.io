University Academic Query Assistant

Built a production-ready Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) assistant for Strathmore University to automate student syllabus and policy inquiries with explainable confidence gating. Engineered a multi-format ingestion pipeline (PDF, DOCX, Tesseract OCR) into ChromaDB with course-code metadata filtering, integrated a CPU-optimized DeBERTa-v3 ONNX topic classifier with local Phi-4-mini inference (and Gemini fallback), and calibrated a composite confidence gate achieving 97% precision across 74% coverage.
